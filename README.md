For this project, I developed a Business Costing App using Kotlin to help small business owners calculate the cost of producing their products and determine suitable selling prices.

The application allows users to enter a product name, the number of units produced, ingredients or materials, labour costs, packaging costs, and other expenses. It calculates the total production cost, cost per unit, suggested selling price based on the desired profit margin, and estimated profit. Users can also save costing records to a text file and view previously saved records.
